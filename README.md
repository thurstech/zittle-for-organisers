# Zittle for Organisers

Run your Zittle events and workshops from a conversation with Claude. Zittle is a booking platform for workshops and events in India, with a booking website for each business.

## Use it

After you add the plugin, connect the Zittle for Organisers connector from the plugin's Connectors tab and sign in with your Zittle account. If you don't have one yet, you can create one there, and your free trial starts as usual. Then ask Claude things like:

- "Set up my pottery studio on Zittle."
- "Create a weekend wheel-throwing workshop at my Madhapur studio, ₹1,800 a seat."
- "Add Saturday sessions at 11am for the next month."
- "How did bookings go this week?"

Claude previews every change and makes it only after you agree. A few choices can't be undone, such as your website address, and Claude asks twice before those. Refunds, payouts, bank details and your Zittle plan stay in your Zittle dashboard.

## What's included

- **A connector** to `https://zittle.in/api/business-mcp`, which acts as your business on Zittle.
- **A skill** that teaches Claude how Zittle works and how to run a business with it: setting up a new business, launching an event, changing the schedule and prices, and a weekly check-in.

## Data

The plugin sends what you ask for to your Zittle account through `zittle.in`, as the business you signed in as, and only with the access you allowed. It stores nothing itself. You can disconnect Claude at any time from **Integrations** in your Zittle dashboard. See the [privacy policy](https://zittle.in/privacy-policy).
